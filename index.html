About Our Website

What is Tri-Blood?

Tri-Blood is an AI-powered image detection system designed to provide rapid identification of three genetic red blood cell disorders: G6PD Deficiency, Sickle Cell Anemia, and Thalassemia. By utilizing high-performance deep learning models, the platform serves as a specialized diagnostic aid to enhance blood transfusion safety.

Why Use It?

Traditional blood screening often relies on manual microscopic evaluation, which is time-consuming, expensive, and prone to human error—especially in regions with limited resources. Tri-Blood automates the detection process, significantly reducing screening time and improving the accuracy and accessibility of services.

How It Works
